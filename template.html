A perfect Saturday morning

A suggested plan built from Rachel's notes. Check winter hours for the market.

  1. 9:00
    Farmers Market at Merchants Square (the square is closed to cars 9am–12pm).
  2. 10:00
    Free organ recital in the Wren Chapel, the oldest academic building in the USA.
  3. 10:30
    Walk the William & Mary campus to the Mason School of Business, then around back to the lake and amphitheatre.
  4. Evening
    A romantic dinner at Le Yaca or Fat Canary.
Hours, seasons and schedules change, so check each website before you go. Map links open a Google Maps search.
