## ⭐ Must-See

If you only have a couple of days, start here.

> **A perfect Saturday morning** (a suggested plan built from my notes; check winter hours for the market)
> - **9:00** Farmers Market at Merchants Square (the square is closed to cars 9am–12pm)
> - **10:00** Free organ recital in the Wren Chapel, the oldest academic building in the USA
> - **10:30** Walk the William & Mary campus to the Mason School of Business, then around back to the lake and amphitheatre
> - **Evening** A romantic dinner at Le Yaca or Fat Canary

#### [Merchants Square](https://www.merchantssquare.org/)

📍 Downtown Williamsburg · [🗺️ Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Merchants+Square+Williamsburg+VA)

- Adorable, with elegant shops and cafes. Many of the fancy restaurants are here. You have to visit at least once!
- The square is blocked off for the Farmers Market 9am–12pm on Saturdays.

<sub>Also in: [Shops & Groceries](#shopping), [Restaurants](#restaurants)</sub>

#### [Williamsburg Farmers Market](https://www.williamsburgfarmersmarket.com/)

📍 Merchants Square, near the Wren Building · [🗺️ Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Williamsburg+Farmers+Market+Merchants+Square)

- Every Saturday morning, 9am–12pm, at Merchants Square, just outside the Wren Building end of campus.
- Check the winter schedule online before you go.

<sub>Also in: [Shops & Groceries](#shopping), [Holiday & Winter](#holiday)</sub>

#### [William & Mary campus](https://www.wm.edu/)

📍 Williamsburg · [🗺️ Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=College+of+William+%26+Mary+Williamsburg+VA)

- The campus is beautiful. You should definitely walk around for a visit.

<sub>Also in: [History in Williamsburg](#history-williamsburg)</sub>

#### [Sir Christopher Wren Building & Wren Chapel](https://www.colonialwilliamsburg.org/locations/wren-building/)

📍 William & Mary · [🗺️ Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Wren+Building+William+%26+Mary)

- The oldest academic building still in use in the USA, so you should go see it.
- Free organ concert in the Wren Chapel every Saturday at 10am (about 30 minutes), played on an 18th-century English chamber organ.
- A perfect pair with the Saturday Farmers Market (9am–12pm) right outside, before or after the recital.

<sub>Also in: [History in Williamsburg](#history-williamsburg), [Music](#music)</sub>

#### [Crim Dell Bridge](https://www.wm.edu/about/visiting/campus-landmarks/crim-dell/index.php)

📍 William & Mary, at the west end of the Sunken Garden · [🗺️ Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Crim+Dell+Bridge+William+%26+Mary+Williamsburg+VA)

- A picturesque wooden bridge over a pond in the woods, one of the prettiest spots on campus.
- Campus legend says that if you kiss at the top of the bridge, you'll be together for life. But cross it alone and you might stay single forever!
- Every spring, graduating seniors cross the bridge together on their way to Commencement.

<sub>Also in: [Romantic & Fancy](#romantic)</sub>

#### [Lake Matoaka & the College Woods](https://www.wm.edu/about/visiting/campus-landmarks/lake-matoaka/)

📍 William & Mary, western edge of campus (behind the Mason School of Business) · [🗺️ Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lake+Matoaka+William+%26+Mary+Williamsburg+VA)

- A peaceful lake surrounded by the College Woods, with more than 10 miles of walking trails. The woods are the largest remaining forest in the city of Williamsburg.
- The lake was made by English colonists in the early 1700s as a mill pond, and is named Matoaka after Pocahontas (Matoaka was her Powhatan name).
- The amphitheatre where the Virginia Symphony plays every year sits on the lake. Swimming isn't allowed, but it's lovely for a walk or a birdwatching stroll.

<sub>Also in: [Beaches & Outdoors](#beaches)</sub>

#### [Mason School of Business](https://mason.wm.edu/)

📍 William & Mary · [🗺️ Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Alan+B.+Miller+Hall+Mason+School+of+Business+Williamsburg)

- The fanciest building on campus and definitely worth a visit.
- Walk behind the building to reach the lake and the amphitheatre by the lake.
- The Virginia Symphony plays a free concert at that amphitheatre every year (see Music).

#### [Colonial Williamsburg](https://www.colonialwilliamsburg.org/)

📍 Richmond Road, Williamsburg · [🗺️ Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sal%27s+by+Victor+1242+Richmond+Rd+Williamsburg+VA)

## 🏛️ History within 2 hours

Worth a day trip, or a stop on the drive to or from DC. Drive times are approximate, from Williamsburg.

#### [Berkeley Plantation](https://www.berkeleyplantation.com/)

📍 Charles City, on the James River (about 40 minutes) · [🗺️ Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Berkeley+Plantation+Charles+City+VA)

- Did you know that the first Thanksgiving in America took place at Berkeley Plantation? Settlers landed here on December 4, 1619, two years before the Pilgrims' feast at Plymouth, and held a thanksgiving to celebrate their safe arrival.
- The Virginia Thanksgiving Festival has been celebrated here since 1958.

<sub>Also in: [Holiday & Winter](#holiday), [Day Trips](#day-trips)</sub>

#### [Chippokes State Park](https://www.dcr.virginia.gov/state-parks/chippokes)

📍 Surry, about 10 minutes from the Jamestown ferry landing · [🗺️ Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Chippokes+State+Park+695+Chippokes+Park+Rd+Surry+VA)

- A state park with a sandy beach on the James River, just across from Jamestown. Take the free ferry over!
- Hunt for fossils on the beach (you can keep shark teeth). It's also one of the oldest continually farmed plantations in the country, farmed since 1619.

<sub>Also in: [Beaches & Outdoors](#beaches), [Day Trips](#day-trips), [Family Fun](#kids)</sub>

#### [Smithfield](https://smithfieldva.gov/297/Visitors)

📍 Isle of Wight County, across the James River (via the ferry or the James River Bridge) · [🗺️ Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Historic+Downtown+Smithfield+VA)

- Home of the famous Smithfield ham! It's known as the "Ham Capital of the World."
- A charming historic river town with a walkable downtown, colorful pig statues to find around town, and restaurants serving Smithfield ham.
- The Isle of Wight County Museum on Main Street is home to the world's oldest ham.

<sub>Also in: [Day Trips](#day-trips), [Shops & Groceries](#shopping), [Restaurants](#restaurants)</sub>

#### [Virginia Symphony at the Lake Matoaka Amphitheatre](https://news.wm.edu/2026/08/13/symphony-under-the-stars-and-by-the-lake/)

📍 William & Mary, by Lake Matoaka (behind the Mason School of Business) · [🗺️ Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Martha+Wren+Briggs+Amphitheatre+Lake+Matoaka+121+Ukrop+Way+Williamsburg+VA)

- Every year the Virginia Symphony Orchestra plays a free "Symphony Under the Stars" concert at William & Mary's outdoor amphitheatre by the lake, usually in late summer or early September.
- Free, but you need a wristband, handed out at the gate before the concert. Go early, because once the wristbands run out the concert is full.

#### [Yorktown Beach & summer festivals](https://www.visityorktown.org/)

📍 Riverwalk Landing, Yorktown (about 20 minutes) · [🗺️ Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Yorktown+Beach+Water+Street+Yorktown+VA)

- A sandy beach on the York River right in Historic Yorktown, next to the shops and restaurants of Riverwalk Landing.
- In summer the waterfront is full of free events: the Sounds of Summer concerts on Thursday evenings (about late May through August), Yorktown Market Days on Saturday mornings, and a July 4th celebration with music on the beach and fireworks over the river.
- In early fall, the free Rhythms on the Riverwalk concerts continue on Thursday evenings. Check the Visit Yorktown calendar for this year's dates.

## 🏖️ Beaches & Outdoors

#### [Lake Matoaka & the College Woods](https://www.wm.edu/about/visiting/campus-landmarks/lake-matoaka/)

📍 William & Mary, western edge of campus (behind the Mason School of Business) · [🗺️ Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lake+Matoaka+William+%26+Mary+Williamsburg+VA)

- A peaceful lake surrounded by the College Woods, with more than 10 miles of walking trails. The woods are the largest remaining forest in the city of Williamsburg.
- The lake was made by English colonists in the early 1700s as a mill pond, and is named Matoaka after Pocahontas (Matoaka was her Powhatan name).
- The amphitheatre where the Virginia Symphony plays every year sits on the lake. Swimming isn't allowed, but it's lovely for a walk or a birdwatching stroll.
